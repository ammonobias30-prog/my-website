FAQ

Questions customers usually ask.

Is the check-up free?

Yes. Zigurado offers a FREE CHECK UP! for customers. Ask our staff about the scope of the check-up for your device.

Do I need to know exactly what is wrong?

No. Describe the symptoms as clearly as you can and our team can check the device.

How long will the repair take?

Repair time depends on the device, problem, parts availability, and required testing. We aim for a quick turnaround whenever possible.

What if another problem is found?

We explain relevant findings and the recommended repair before proceeding with work outside the agreed repair.

How long is the warranty?

LCD and charging board repairs have a 1-week warranty. Battery repairs have a 1-month warranty, subject to the shop's warranty terms.
